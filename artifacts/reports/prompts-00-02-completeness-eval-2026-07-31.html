Why PROMPT-02 fails its own acceptance criteria

The spec says: “reconcile canonical state through the Info API after reconnect; de-duplicate replayed records” and “the bound thread renders live price, equity, and the market snapshot with freshness stamps.” The adapter can fetch all of this, but nothing wires the live stream into the UI or reconciles it. The WebSocket client reconnects and resubscribes, but no consumer reads its output. The card is a placeholder. So reads work on-demand via tools, but the always-on reconciled view the prompt describes is not realized.
