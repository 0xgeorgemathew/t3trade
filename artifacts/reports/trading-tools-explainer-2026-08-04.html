Mission & Strategy Tools

📋
trading_get_mission
Phase 1

The entry point for every turn. Read the complete mission state: status, authority (risk limits and capital allocation), the active momentum strategy, registered watches, user control flags, and any executions still in flight. The withdrawable field tells you what's actually free to trade right now — the mandate ceilings are hard limits that never move with the balance.

trading_publish_momentum_strategy
Phase 1

Publish or switch the momentum strategy. Supply expectedVersion from your last trading_get_mission call (0 before first publish). A stale version is rejected with the current version — this prevents concurrent writes from clobbering each other. Publishing supersedes all watches from the previous version; re-arm what you still need. Cancel resting orders from the old thesis manually with trading_request_entry.

Required Field
Every entry in strategy.entryPlan.conditions[] MUST have a non-empty description field. You may also pass conditions as bare prose strings.

trading_get_market_history
Phase 2

Read bounded OHLCV candle history. Intervals: 1m, 3m, 5m, 15m, or 1h (no local synthesis — §13 forbids synthesizing intervals from smaller ones). One response caps at 500 bars. Form a thesis on enough bars to see the structure you're claiming.

Recommendation
Fewer than ~60 bars on the mission's timeframe is a guess about a chart you haven't looked at.

Account & Position Tools

trading_get_account_state
Phase 2

Read the canonical account state: total account value, margin used, withdrawable collateral, and all open positions. Uses the master-wallet address as identity (the execution wallet signs but is never used as account identity). withdrawable is what's actually free to trade — the mandate ceilings in authority don't move with the balance.

Important
withdrawable is the live balance, not your mandate. Mandate ceilings are hard limits that never adjust when the account value moves.

Watch Tools

trading_register_watch
Phase 3

Register a typed market watch bound to the current strategy version. The watch fires when its predicate matches and then becomes terminal (fires exactly once). Watches do NOT survive a strategy publish — publishing supersedes all watches from the previous version. Re-arm what you still need after publishing.

Critical Rules
A watch fires once and is terminal. Re-register after it fires to keep it standing. Publishing a new strategy supersedes all watches — re-arm what you still need.

Phase 4

Execution Tool

🎯
trading_request_entry
Phase 4

Submit one execution intent. This is the complete position lifecycle — not just entries. The actionType selects the operation: open, scale_in, reduce, close, cancel, or modify_stop. Position-increasing actions require a valid stop. There is no market order — use marketable_ioc order preference for immediate execution.

Validation Checklist
Before signing: mission must be active, strategy version current, market = ETH, position-increasing intents need a valid stop, loss budget must admit it, exchange reads must be fresh, and NO other execution can be mid-submission.
Exhaustion Rule
reduce, close, cancel, and modify_stop remain available even when the loss budget is exhausted.
